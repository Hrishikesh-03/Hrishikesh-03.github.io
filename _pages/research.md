<span class="project-badge">Master's Thesis &bull; Jan 2026 – Present</span>

### Exploring Non-Hermitian Physics and Exceptional Points in Cold Atoms
**Supervisor:** Dr. Ashok Mohapatra | *National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER)*

* **Optical Infrastructure & Recommissioning:** Recommissioned an existing $^{87}\text{Rb}$ cold-atom setup by designing and building the complete optical cooling and repumper beamlines, saturated absorption spectroscopy frequency stabilization locks, polarization-control optics, and multi-axis absorption imaging systems.

![Rb-87 Setup Schematic](images/research/mot_chamber.svg)

* **Cooling & Atomic Cloud Optimization:** Established the $^{87}\text{Rb}$ Magneto-Optical Trap (MOT) and implemented sub-Doppler optical molasses cooling to maximize cloud phase-space density and minimize thermal expansion.

* **Current Experimental Direction:** Engineering an effective non-Hermitian Hamiltonian in cold atomic vapors utilizing polarization-dependent atom-light interactions (Faraday and Voigt effects). Currently conducting polarimetric measurements to detect qualitative transmission and birefringence signatures near exceptional points in polarization space.

* **Physical Objective:** Investigating topological mode-switching and examining the predicted boundaries of enhanced parameter sensitivity around non-Hermitian exceptional points.
